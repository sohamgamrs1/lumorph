Lumorph

Privacy

What we keep, and where.

Short version: your data lives in your browser. There is no Lumorph server holding it.

What is stored

Your profile answers — name, gender, age, height, skin type and focus — plus the score and pillar values of each scan, a small thumbnail of each scan, your quest history and your consent record. All of it is in this browser's local storage, on this device.

What is not stored

The 478 face landmarks are computed and discarded in the same moment. They are never written to disk. There is no account, no login and no server-side database.

The optional AI read

Off by default. While it is on, each scan sends one photo to Google Gemini to get a model estimate of attractiveness, harmony and skin. The photo is sent over HTTPS, used for that one answer, and not stored by Lumorph — there is no Lumorph server to store it on. Google's handling is governed by their API terms. Turn it off and no photo leaves this device again.

Analytics

Lumorph does not run analytics by default. If measurement is enabled on this deployment, it counts page views only — never photos, scores, or anything you type. Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland are asked first, and nothing is loaded until they agree.

Security and abuse prevention

When the AI read is used, the request passes through a rate limiter that looks at your IP address for a few minutes to stop the service being spammed and to protect the owner's API key. The address is held in memory only: never written to a database, never stored beside a photo, and discarded when the window closes.

Request bodies are not kept. The photo exists in memory for the length of a single request and is then dropped. Nothing is written to a log.

Uploads are checked before they leave your device: only JPEG, PNG and WebP under 12MB are accepted, and the copy sent for an AI read is resized to 768 pixels first.

Third parties

Two public resources are downloaded once and cached: the MediaPipe face landmarker and the two typefaces. Both are served by their own CDNs, which see an ordinary web request. After that the app runs offline.

Deleting

Settings, then Delete all data removes everything immediately and permanently. Clearing your browser's site data does the same. Export first if you want a copy — Settings has JSON and CSV exports.

Children

Lumorph is not intended for anyone under 13, and the app will not accept an age below 13.

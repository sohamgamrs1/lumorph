What is Lumorph?

A lookmaxxing app that measures facial proportions from a photo and turns them into a score you can track over time, plus a routine and daily habits built around the result. It runs in the browser, on your own device.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising. The scoring runs on your phone, so there is nothing for us to bill you for.

Does it upload my photos?

Not by default. The face landmarks are found and discarded on your device, and only the scores are stored, in your browser. The optional AI read is the one exception: if you turn it on, one photo per scan is sent to Google Gemini. It is off until you enable it.

How is the score calculated?

478 points are found on your face, and 22 measurements are taken from them — symmetry, facial thirds and fifths, eye span, midface ratio, jaw width, gonial angle, canthal tilt, lip ratios and more. Each is compared with a reference band and weighted into five pillars.

Why did my score change between photos?

Head angle, distance, lighting and expression all move the numbers. Compare scans taken the same way: same room, same light, phone at eye level. The scanner checks angle, light and focus before it will capture, which reduces the problem but cannot remove it.

Is this medical advice?

No. Lumorph is an appearance tool. It does not diagnose, treat or screen for any condition, and it must never be used to check for acne, moles or skin cancer. Speak to a doctor or dermatologist for anything medical.

What is the AI read?

An optional second opinion. Geometry measures proportion; it cannot tell a handsome face with bad skin from an unremarkable one with good bones. A vision model can, so when you enable it, one photo is sent to Google and the result is blended with the geometric score.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Settings, then Delete all data. Every scan, photo, quest and profile answer is erased from your browser immediately. There is no copy anywhere else.

Does it work without a camera?

Yes. Upload a photo instead — the same measurements run on it.

Is it for a specific gender?

No. The reference ranges differ by gender, and the tier names differ too, but the app works for everyone.

Still stuck? Open the app and read "How these numbers are calculated" in Settings.
